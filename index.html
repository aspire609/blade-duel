Blade Aura: Duel

Your mouse is the sword arm. There are no buttons to slash: the blade has weight, and its speed on impact is your damage. Whip it fast, and hit enemies mid wind-up to break their attacks.

Mouse
Move the blade (faster = harder hit)
W A S D
Move, Shift to sprint
Q E
Turn (you auto-face the nearest foe)
Space
Dodge roll, brief invulnerability
